# Day 1: Understand Business Problem and Analysis Objectives

## Project: Sales and Business Analytics Dashboard



### 1\. Introduction

Business Analytics is the process of collecting, organizing, and analyzing business data to understand performance, identify problems, and support better decision-making.

In this project, we will analyze sales data and present important business insights through an interactive dashboard.



### 2\. Business Problem

Businesses need to understand how well they are performing. However, they may face challenges such as:

* Decreasing sales over time.
* Products or categories with low sales.
* Low profit or declining profit margins.
* Differences in sales performance across regions.
* Changes in customer purchasing patterns.
* High discounts that may reduce profitability.

**Example:** A company notices that its sales are decreasing. It wants to identify which products, categories, or regions are contributing to the decline so that it can take appropriate action.



### 3\. Analysis Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze total sales and total profit.
2. Identify top-performing and low-performing products.
3. Compare sales and profit across different regions.
4. Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
5. Understand customer purchasing patterns.
6. Examine the effect of discounts on sales and profit.
7. Present useful insights that can support business decisions.

### 4\. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

KPIs are measurable values used to track business performance.

|KPI|Purpose|
|-|-|
|Total Sales|Measures the total revenue generated from sales.|
|Total Profit|Measures the profit earned by the business.|
|Total Orders|Shows the number of orders received.|
|Profit Margin|Shows the percentage of sales retained as profit.|
|Average Order Value (AOV)|Shows the average value of each order.|
|Sales Growth|Tracks how sales change over a period of time.|

### 

### 5\. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is an interactive Sales and Business Analytics Dashboard that will help users:

* Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify high-performing and low-performing products.
* Compare business performance across regions and time periods.
* Understand important trends and patterns.
* Make informed, data-driven business decisions.



### 6\. Conclusion

Understanding the business problem and defining clear analysis objectives are the first steps in a data analytics project. These steps help us decide what data to analyze, which KPIs to calculate, and what information the dashboard should communicate.
