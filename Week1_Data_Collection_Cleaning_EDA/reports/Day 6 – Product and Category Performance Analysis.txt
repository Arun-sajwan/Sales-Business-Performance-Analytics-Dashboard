## 7. Overall Business Insights

1. Technology is the leading category in both sales and profit.
2. Chairs and Phones are the strongest sub-categories based on sales.
3. Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the top-selling product.
4. Furniture has high sales but comparatively low profit.
5. Fasteners, Labels, and Envelopes have the lowest sub-category sales.
6. The business should investigate product demand and profitability to improve overall performance.

## 8. Recommendations

- Focus on maintaining the performance of high-selling products.
- Develop suitable marketing strategies for low-performing sub-categories.
- Investigate Furniture category costs, discounts, and profit margins.
- Review inventory levels based on product demand.
- Analyze product-level profit before making pricing and promotional decisions.

## 9. Conclusion

Product and category performance analysis helps businesses understand sales trends, identify profitable categories, and recognize low-performing products. Technology is the strongest category in this analysis, while Furniture needs further profitability investigation. These findings can support better inventory planning, marketing strategies, and business decisions.
